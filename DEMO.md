# EarthEcho: 5-minute walkthrough

Open https://earth-echo-nine.vercel.app/ on a phone or laptop. Language and dark mode are at the top. Everything below works in English and Bengali.

1. **Pick a place (30 s).** Tap "Use my location", or tap the globe and zoom to a field. Confirm the pin. The app now prepares weather, soil and NASA data for that spot.
2. **Weather (45 s).** See today, the 16-day forecast, farm alerts and calm hours for spraying. Open the local news box (Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Nepal, Myanmar, Bhutan, USA).
3. **Disaster watch (30 s).** One hazard level from earthquakes, UN alerts, NASA events and river flood forecast, with safety steps.
4. **Irrigation (45 s).** Choose a crop and method. The schedule shows when to water and how much, and how much water a better method saves.
5. **Rotation planner (45 s).** Pick irrigation and a crop. The app shows year plans that fit the place, why, and a profit estimate you can edit.
6. **Market prices and Suppliers (30 s).** Recent prices from UN WFP data; nearby agro shops on a map.
7. **My farms (30 s).** Save the place. Add another place. See each farm's alerts. On a phone, install the app and open it offline.
8. **Ask EarthEcho (60 s).** Try "Which crops suit my farm now?" and "Is it a good time to spray tomorrow?". Open the Sources list under the answer to see where each statement came from.
9. **Suitability, Soil, NASA environment, Data (as time allows).** Every number shows its source and licence.

Things worth knowing when judging: every output is labelled as an estimate with its source; when data is missing the app says so instead of guessing; farm data stays on the device.
